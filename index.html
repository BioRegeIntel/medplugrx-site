The Standard

Measured,
Not Claimed

We are a distributor, and we are a selective one. Every lot we carry arrives with a certificate of analysis from the processing laboratory — exosome count and full size distribution measured on that specific lot, never averaged from a batch. If the record does not match the vial, we do not take the lot.

I

Verified Source

We buy only from accredited United States processing facilities operating under published quality, testing and biobanking frameworks — and we read the audit file before we place an order.

II

Counted Per Lot

The figure on the label is the figure measured on that lot, reported on the certificate of analysis that travels with it. Nothing is averaged, rounded up, or carried over from a previous run.

III

Unbroken Custody

Consented donor through validated cold chain, documented end to end. A lot with a gap anywhere in that record is a lot we turn away rather than pass along.

Allocation

Supplied By Register,
Not By Volume

Accounts are verified against state licensure before a single vial moves. We do not sell to consumers, we do not list on marketplaces, and we do not discount to win an order. The physicians on our register are supplied first, supplied completely, and never supplied a substitute.
